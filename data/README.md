# The case: Athenova's "Learn Smarter" campaign

> **Athenova is fictional.** All data is synthetic and generated by `generate_sample_data.py` (fixed seed, so it is reproducible).
> No real company, person, or personal data is involved.

**Athenova** is a Series-A EdTech startup whose app, *Athenova Pro*, is an AI tutor that explains concepts step by step
for high-school and college students. For the 2026 back-to-school season the company runs **"Learn Smarter"**:

| | |
|---|---|
| Dates | 12 weeks, Mon Aug 3 – Sun Oct 25, 2026 |
| Paid-media plan | **$1.2M** ($100k/week: TikTok $35k, Instagram $30k, YouTube $20k, LinkedIn $15k) |
| Audiences | **Students** (users), **Parents** (payers), **Educators** (B2B school licenses) |
| Funnel | impressions → clicks → 14-day free trials → paying subscribers; on LinkedIn also → school demo requests |
| Economics (assumptions) | $96 first-year revenue per subscriber; $1,500 expected pipeline value per school demo request |

## What happened (what students should discover)

1. **CPA ≠ CAC.** TikTok buys the cheapest trials (≈$10) but not the cheapest customers. Students try, parents pay.
2. **B2B is different.** LinkedIn looks terrible on CAC and ROAS but generates school demo requests worth about $8 of pipeline per $1.
3. **Content matters by audience.** Creator/UGC content wins on TikTok and Instagram, testimonials convert parents, thought leadership drives demo requests, and promos flop on LinkedIn.
4. **Week 7 privacy incident.** A viral post claims Athenova trains its AI on student homework. Privacy complaints spike, and parent and educator conversion roughly halves. The team launches "Trust & privacy" content in week 8 and conversion recovers.
5. **Diminishing returns.** From week 9 the team pushes TikTok 60% over plan: CPM rises, CTR falls, CAC roughly doubles, and the campaign ends about 6% over budget.
6. **A/B test (weeks 4–5, TikTok).** Hook B ("Go from a B to an A this semester") wins on CTR (p < .001), but hook A ("Save 5 hours of homework a week") converts clicks to trials better (p ≈ .004). Trials per impression are statistically tied. B also makes an efficacy claim that may be unsubstantiated.

**KPI glossary:** CPM = spend / impressions × 1000 · CTR = clicks / impressions · CPA = spend / trials ·
CAC = spend / paying customers · ROAS = first-year revenue / spend · engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares) / impressions.
