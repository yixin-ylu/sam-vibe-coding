The case: Smiley's "Learn Smarter" campaign

Smiley (fictional) is an AI-tutoring startup launching Smiley Pro with a 12-week, $1.2M back-to-school campaign on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn. It targets three audiences: students (users), parents (payers) and educators (B2B school licenses). The synthetic data tracks every post from impression to free trial to paying customer or school demo request.

CPA ≠ CAC

TikTok buys the cheapest trials ($10) but not the cheapest customers: parents cost $58, students $225.

B2B is different

LinkedIn looks terrible on CAC but returns about $8 of school-license pipeline per $1.

A trust crisis

In week 7 a viral post claims Smiley trains its AI on student homework. Parent conversion halves. Can students spot it and quantify it?

Overspend & a risky A/B test

TikTok runs 60% over plan and CAC doubles. Ad hook B wins clicks but loses trials, and makes an efficacy claim that may be unsubstantiated.

Getting started

  1. Click any Open in Colab link and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Run the first cell. It copies the course files into your session.
  3. Demos 1–5 need no key. For 6, add a free GEMINI_API_KEY in Colab's 🔑 Secrets panel.
  4. For Demo 7, add an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (about $0.15–$0.60 per run).
